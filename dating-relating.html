Room for an honest conversation.

Working closely with people has taught me how much changes when trust is established and the usual filters come down. We can speak more honestly about what we want, what feels difficult, and the patterns we keep returning to.

Dating and relating is another part of my work: listening, asking questions, and offering an independent perspective with your best interests at heart. The starting point is your experience and what you want to understand.

If there’s something in your dating life or relationships you’d like to talk through, get in touch. We can discuss what you’re looking for and whether working together feels right.
