Another branch of my work: bringing attention, imagination and care to the spaces we grow.

A place for things to grow.

The Ministry of Gardening Affairs is my gardening project. It brings the same curiosity and care I bring to working with people into a different setting: gardens and the possibilities they hold.

I’m interested in what happens when we give a place our attention, work with what is already there, and make room for something new. Observation, creativity and practical care all have a part to play.

Have a garden, a growing-space idea, or a project you’d like to explore together? I’m open for business. Tell me what you have in mind and we can discuss how I might help.
